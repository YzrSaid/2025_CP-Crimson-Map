About Crimson Map

Crimson Map is an innovative augmented reality navigation solution built specifically to help freshmen and visitors navigate Western Mindanao State University. With comprehensive coverage across WMSU Campus A, B, and C, the app provides seamless wayfinding through interactive AR overlays and turn-by-turn guidance.

Whether you're looking for a specific classroom, administrative office, or campus facility, Crimson Map ensures smooth campus exploration with intuitive navigation, real-time location tracking, and offline map support. The app is optimized for Android devices to provide the best user experience.

How to Use Crimson Map

1

Download & Install

Download the Crimson Map APK from our website and install it on your Android device. Make sure to enable installation from unknown sources in your settings.

2

Open the App

Launch Crimson Map and wait for the app to load. You'll be taken to the landing page where the app begins locating your position using GPS.

3

Lock Your Location

The app automatically detects your GPS location. Click the lock button to confirm your position. If GPS is slow, scan any QR code placed around WMSU buildings to instantly lock your location.

4

Search & Select Destination

Use the search bar to find your destination building or facility. Select it from the list, and the app will automatically calculate the best route for you.

5

Navigate with AR

Start navigating and view your route through AR. Buildings appear as labeled nodes, and directional arrows guide you with instructions like "Turn Left" or "Go Straight." Your route is also visible on the map view.

6

Arrive & Explore Rooms

When you reach your destination, stop the navigation. A button will appear on the map that you can click to view all rooms and facilities inside the building.
